How it works

Two sides, one simple handover.

StallSpace gets you found and gets the booking down in writing. The two of you take it from there — on WhatsApp, like you already would.

If you’re buying

Find it, book it, done.

  1. Browse stalls in your area — by category, or just search.
  2. Pick a slot or add things to a basket. Your slot is held from the moment you send the request, so nobody books over you.
  3. WhatsApp opens with the booking written out. You press send — StallSpace never messages anyone for you.
  4. The vendor confirms in the app, and you pay them directly.

Free to browse, and you can look around without an account.

If you’re selling

Set up once, get found after.

  1. Create your stall — name, area, what you offer, a few photos.
  2. List services with a price and how long they take, or products with a price and stock.
  3. Requests land in your dashboard, with a bell and an email. Confirm or decline in one tap.
  4. Mark it paid when the money lands. Your earnings add up month by month.

Your WhatsApp number stays private until someone actually books with you.

Questions

The things everyone asks first.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It’s free to join, free to list, and StallSpace takes no commission on anything you sell. There are no fees to browse or book either.

How do I get paid?

Directly, by the customer — EFT, cash, SnapScan, Zapper, Capitec Pay, whatever the two of you agree. StallSpace doesn’t process payments and never holds your money, so there’s nothing to wait for and nothing to withdraw.

You mark a booking as paid in the app when the money lands, and it counts toward your monthly earnings.

Does StallSpace message the vendor for me?

No — and we’re deliberate about that. When you book, WhatsApp opens with all the details already typed out, and you press send. Nothing goes out in your name without you doing it.

The vendor also sees the request in their dashboard straight away, so it reaches them either way.

Is my WhatsApp number public?

No. Your number is only shared with someone once you have a confirmed booking or order with them. It never appears on a public stall page, and it’s not visible to people just browsing.

Do I need to be a registered business?

No. StallSpace is built for side hustles — someone doing nails between lectures, a weekend baker, a tutor, a thrifter, a photographer, a small shop that’s been going for years. All of it belongs here.

What does “beta” mean here?

The product is built and working — you can set up a stall and take real bookings today. Beta means we’re still early, still adding to it, and still shaping it around what the first vendors actually need. If something’s missing, tell us and it gets looked at properly.

What if something goes wrong with a booking?

Because payment happens directly between you and the other person, StallSpace isn’t a middleman who can refund you. What we do give you is a record of what was agreed, and a report button — reports go to a moderation queue, and stalls that abuse the platform get suspended and removed from Discover.
